Super Over rules used when the scorer starts a Super Over after a tie

"Wickets allowed" = the innings ends when this many wickets fall (e.g. 2). When a match ends in a tie, the scorer chooses Start Super Over or Finish as tie. If the Super Over is tied, the team with more boundaries (4s + 6s) in its Super Over innings wins; if those are level too, the scorer records the coin toss winner.
